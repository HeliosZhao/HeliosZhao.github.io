About me

I am currently a Ph.D. student in the Computer Vision and Robotic Perception (CVRP) Laboratory at the National University of Singapore, under the supervision of A/P Gim Hee Lee. I received my B.E. degree from Tianjin University in 2020.

My research interests include person re-identification, semantic segmentation and transfer learning.
