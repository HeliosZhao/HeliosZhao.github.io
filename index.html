Yuyang Zhao

I am currently a Ph.D. candidate in the Computer Vision and Robotic Perception (CVRP) Laboratory at National University of Singapore, under the supervision of A/P Gim Hee Lee. I received my B.E. degree from Tianjin University in 2020.

My research interests lie in building generalizable computer vision systems, especially securing model performance under distribution and category shifts. Currently, I am focusing on generalizable semantic segmentation.
